ABOUT NyxApse

THE MISSION

NyxApse (pronounced NIK-saps) is designed to sharpen your cognitive reflexes, mathematical speed, focus, and memory. By engaging in daily "trainings," you build Brain Power and master your mental discipline.

HOW IT WORKS

🧠 Games: Earn Brain Power (🧠) by playing Math, Dots, Memory Matrix, Focus, Aim, or Reflex.

🔥 Daily streak: Play every day for a bonus on your first win (streak days × 10 BP, up to 70). Use ◀ ▶ in any game to replay a lower level.

🎯 Daily challenge: One game is picked each day. Win it for +100 BP.

🏅 Achievements: Earn badges for wins, streaks, levels and skins. They show on the home screen.

⚡ Best times: Each level remembers your fastest clear. Use ⏸ to pause and ? for the rules of any game.

📈 Progression: Unlimited levels. As the level increases, the speed and difficulty scale up to keep your brain challenged.

🎨 Customization: Spend your Brain Power (🧠) in the Skin Shop to unlock new theme colors that apply to the entire platform.

PRO TIPS

  • Consistency: Playing 5 minutes every day is better than 1 hour once a week.
  • Mastery: Reach 50,000 Brain Power (🧠) to unlock the legendary Rainbow Skin!
  • High Levels: Each win gives Level × 5 BP (🧠) up to level 30. After that, rewards keep growing but more slowly.

    DATA STORAGE

    Your progress, unlocked levels, and purchased skins are saved locally in your browser (LocalStorage). Note: Clearing your browser cache or site data will reset your progress.

    💾 Backup & transfer: Open the Skin Shop on the home screen and tap Export save to copy a save code. Keep it as a backup, or paste it with Import save on another phone or computer to continue where you left off. Importing replaces the progress on that device.
