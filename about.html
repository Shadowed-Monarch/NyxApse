ABOUT NyxApse

THE MISSION

NyxApse is designed to sharpen your cognitive reflexes, mathematical speed, focus, and memory. By engaging in daily "trainings," you build Brain Power and master your mental discipline.

HOW IT WORKS

🧠 Games: Earn Brain Power (🧠) by playing Math, Dots, Memory Matrix, Focus, Aim, Reflex, or Odd One Out.

🔥 Daily streak: Win a level every day to grow your streak and fill the calendar on the home screen. Your first win each day pays a bonus of streak days × 10 coins (up to 70). Use ◀ ▶ in any game to replay a lower level.

🎯 Daily challenge: One game is picked each day. Win it 2 to 4 times for +60 coins.

🪙 Coins vs Brain Power: Brain Power is your score and rank, and it never goes down. Coins are what you spend in the Skin Shop.

🏅 Achievements: Earn badges for wins, streaks, levels and skins. Each one pays 25 coins and shows on the home screen.

⚡ Best times: Each level remembers your fastest clear. Use ⏸ to pause and ? for the rules of any game.

⚙️ Settings & profile: Open the gear on any page to change sound, looks (including a Minimal style), and the seconds for each game. Choose a nickname and avatar on the home screen. The focus sound keeps playing as you move between pages.

📈 Progression: Unlimited levels. As the level increases, the speed and difficulty scale up to keep your brain challenged.

🎨 Customization: Spend your coins (🪙) in the Skin Shop to unlock new theme colors that apply to the entire platform.

PRO TIPS

  • Consistency: Playing 5 minutes every day is better than 1 hour once a week.
  • Mastery: Save up 10,000 coins (🪙) to unlock the legendary Rainbow Skin!
  • High Levels: Each win gives Level × 5 BP (🧠) up to level 30. After that, rewards keep growing but more slowly. Each win also pays 5 + your level coins (up to 55).

    DATA STORAGE

    Your progress, unlocked levels, and purchased skins are saved locally in your browser (LocalStorage). Note: Clearing your browser cache or site data will reset your progress.

    💾 Backup & transfer: Open the Skin Shop on the home screen and tap Export save to copy a save code. Keep it as a backup, or paste it with Import save on another phone or computer to continue where you left off. Importing replaces the progress on that device.
