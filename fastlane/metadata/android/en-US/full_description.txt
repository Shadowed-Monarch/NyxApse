NyxApse is a collection of fast mini games for practicing speed, focus and memory. It works fully offline, has no ads and no tracking, and is open source (MIT).

GAMES
- Math: solve falling equations before time runs out
- Dots: tap the numbered dots in order
- Memory: remember the glowing blocks
- Focus: pick the colour of the word, not the word
- Aim: pop the falling bubbles
- Reflex: dodge the falling bubbles
- Odd One Out: spot the shape that is different

FEATURES
- Endless levels that get harder as you improve, with Brain Power ranks from Student to Overlord
- Coins to spend on 27 skins, including animated ones
- Daily challenge, streak calendar and 12 badges
- Settings for sound (brown, pink or white noise), looks (including a flat, fast Minimal style) and game timers
- Optional online leaderboard (off by default). It needs internet, uses a hosted Supabase service, and only shares a nickname, an avatar and your win records.

NyxApse is for fun and practice. It makes no medical or health claims.
