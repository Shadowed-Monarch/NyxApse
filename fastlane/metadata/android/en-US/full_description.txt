NyxApse is a collection of fast mini games for practicing speed, focus and memory. It works fully offline, has no ads and no tracking, and is open source (MIT).

GAMES
- Grammar: pick the word that fills the gap
- Math: solve falling equations before time runs out
- Dots: tap the numbered dots in order
- Memory: remember the glowing blocks
- Focus: pick the colour of the word, not the word
- Aim: pop the falling bubbles
- Reflex: dodge the falling bubbles
- Odd One Out: spot the shape that is different
- N-Back: tap when a square is where it was N turns ago
- Sequence: watch the squares light up, then repeat the order

FEATURES
- Endless levels that get harder as you improve, with 16 Brain Power ranks from Student to Nyxborn
- Coins to spend on 32 skins, including animated ones
- Daily challenge (2 or 3 games a day), streak calendar and 30 badges
- 50 drawn profile icons (dragons, ghosts and more), plus emoji avatars
- Settings for sound (brown, pink or white noise), looks (including a flat, fast Minimal style, a black and white switch and a colorblind-friendly mode)
- Smooth menu and page-slide animations (off in Minimal style and with reduced motion)
- Optional Cloud save for players 16 and older: restore your progress with a 10-digit code after clearing data or changing phone
- Pause any game with Space or P on a keyboard
- Optional online leaderboard (off by default, for players 16 and older): weekly divisions from Bronze to Legend, seasons, and public profiles. It needs internet and uses a hosted Supabase service. It only shares a nickname, an avatar, your division and your win records.

NyxApse is for fun and practice. It makes no medical or health claims.
