PRIVACY NyxApse

THE SHORT VERSION

NyxApse has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device unless you choose to join the leaderboard.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your progress (Brain Power, coins, levels, skins, streak, badges), your settings, your nickname and avatar, and a log of your wins are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or using Reset in Settings deletes it. If you tap "Scan QR code" when restoring progress, the app asks to use your camera. The picture is read on your device only and is never recorded or sent.

IF YOU JOIN THE LEADERBOARD

Leaderboards are optional and off by default. If you switch them on, NyxApse sends this to its server (hosted on Supabase): your nickname, your avatar, a random anonymous player ID, and your win records (game, level, time taken, Brain Power earned). The server also keeps your division, your season history, your best level in each game and your total wins. Other players who open the leaderboard can see a public profile with your nickname, avatar, division, best division, best levels and total wins. Nothing else is shown, and you can report a profile. Switching the leaderboard off and deleting your leaderboard data removes all of it, including your public profile. The leaderboard is for players aged 16 and older (or older where the law in your country requires it).

It does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location. Like any online service, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service. The data is used only to show the leaderboard and to spot cheating.

YOUR CHOICES

You can switch the leaderboard off at any time in Settings. Use Settings → Leaderboard → Delete my leaderboard data to remove your nickname and win records from the server.

Please don't use your real name as a nickname.

CLOUD SAVE (OPTIONAL)

Cloud save is off by default. If you switch it on (players aged 16 and older), your game progress is stored on the server: levels, coins, skins, badges, settings, nickname and avatar. It is found with your nickname and a 10-digit code that the app creates on your device. Only a scrambled hash of the code is stored, not the code itself. Login tokens are never included. Wrong guesses lock a nickname for an hour. Turning Cloud save off, or deleting your leaderboard data, deletes your cloud copy, and copies not used for about 400 days are deleted automatically. Without the code and your nickname, nobody can restore your save, and nobody (including the author) can recover a lost code.

NICKNAMES

Nicknames are checked by an automatic filter in the app and on the server. It blocks offensive words, impersonation of staff, and contact details such as links or long numbers. Please do not use your real name.
