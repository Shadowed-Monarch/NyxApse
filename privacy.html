PRIVACY NyxApse

THE SHORT VERSION

NyxApse has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device unless you choose to join the leaderboard.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your progress (Brain Power, coins, levels, skins, streak, badges), your settings, your nickname and avatar, and a log of your wins are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or using Reset in Settings deletes it.

IF YOU JOIN THE LEADERBOARD

Leaderboards are optional and off by default. If you switch them on, NyxApse sends this to its server (hosted on Supabase): your nickname, your avatar, a random anonymous player ID, and your win records (game, level, time taken, Brain Power earned).

It does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location. Like any online service, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service. The data is used only to show the leaderboard and to spot cheating.

YOUR CHOICES

You can switch the leaderboard off at any time in Settings. Use Settings → Leaderboard → Delete my leaderboard data to remove your nickname and win records from the server.

Please don't use your real name as a nickname.
